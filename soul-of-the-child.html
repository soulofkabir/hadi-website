Rome, 1924
"When I was in Italy, Mme. Montessori told me that besides all the activities she gives to children, she encourages them to keep silence; and after a little time, they like it so much that they prefer silence to their activity. And it interested me to see a little girl of about six years of age, when the time of silence came, went and closed the windows and door, and put away all the things that she was playing with. Then she came and sat in her little chair and closed her eyes… It seemed she preferred those five minutes of silence to all the playing of the whole day."
Hazrat Inayat Khan, from Tales

What is documented, and what is resonance

Keeping the history clear makes the spiritual comparison stronger, not weaker. The traditions remain distinct; they meet in shared questions.

1924

Montessori & Inayat Khan

An eyewitness account in Inayat Khan's Complete Works describes their meeting in Rome, her demonstration of the method, and his admiration for the silence exercise.

1939

Montessori & Theosophy

Maria and Mario Montessori travel to India at the invitation of the Theosophical Society and teach at Adyar.

1939–46

Cosmic Education deepens

Kept in India by the war, Montessori and Mario further develop the Cosmic Education plan for the elementary years.

✦

Inayat Khan & Theosophy

Inayat Khan lectured and performed on the Mysticism of Sound at venues including London's Theosophical Society.

Sources: Association Montessori Internationale timeline; The Theosophical Society, Adyar; Complete Works of Hazrat Inayat Khan (1924); Inayatiyya.
